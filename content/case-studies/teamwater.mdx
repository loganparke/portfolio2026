## Problem

I shipped the creator-recruitment landing page for MrBeast's #TeamWater charity campaign. The campaign raised $40M in a month.

## Role

Revmatics handed me a brand book, the campaign logo, the fonts, and a designer to partner with. MrBeast's team handled the donation processor. The page I built was for recruiting other creators to help fundraise.

## Approach

Custom layout on top of the page builder, custom animations, GTM and meta tag wiring, domain and subdomain setup. We shipped within a few days of getting the brief.

What mattered most was performance. Mobile and desktop both had to load fast under campaign-scale traffic, and the Lighthouse scores had to back that up. Another engineer set up dedicated hosting capacity for the traffic spikes.

Mid-campaign updates were small. Copy changes as the creator count climbed, refreshed hero imagery as new assets came in.

## Outcome

Tens of thousands of pageviews a day during the campaign. Nothing broke.

## Stack

JavaScript, custom animations, Google Tag Manager.
